## Core Production Process

### Step 1: Idea Expansion

Expand a simple concept into a fully cinematic scene including environment,
lighting, camera movement, and emotional tone.

### Step 2: Prompt Engineering

Specify:

- Camera angle and lens behavior
- Lighting style
- Motion behavior
- Scene environment
- Texture realism
- Animation pacing
- Brand tone
- Visual atmosphere

### Step 3: Scene Cinematic Direction

Treat each video like a film scene, defining:

- Shot composition
- Subject focus
- Depth layering
- Motion choreography
- Visual storytelling flow

### Step 4: Iteration and Refinement

Refine prompts to:

- Increase realism
- Improve cinematic quality
- Maintain brand accuracy
- Enhance engagement and retention
- Ensure visual clarity for AI generation tools
